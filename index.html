14 — Questions, answered
Do you build websites and run social, or just one?
Both — plus GEO. Most clients start with a website build or a social retainer, then expand to full-stack within 90 days because the results compound when the three work together.
What's the difference between SEO and GEO?
SEO optimizes for a position on a results page. GEO optimizes for a mention inside an AI-generated answer. The two work together — but GEO is what drives AI-referred leads, which convert 3× better than search traffic.
How long until I see results?
Website: launch in 4–6 weeks. Social: engagement lift within 30 days, revenue impact in 60–90. GEO: structural fixes shift citations within 2–4 weeks, and content authority compounds over 2–3 months.
Can you work with my existing site?
Yes. If your site is solid we layer GEO and social on top — schema markup, restructuring key pages, and earning citations. If it needs a rebuild, we'll tell you in the audit.
What do I actually get in the free audit?
We run the prompts your customers are already asking through ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, review your site's structured data coverage, and scan your social presence. You get a written report in 48 hours. No pitch deck, no obligation.
Do you work with multi-location businesses?
Yes — that's our Enterprise tier. Per-location schema, distinct service descriptions per market, and market-by-market citation tracking.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Website builds are one-time projects. Social and GEO retainers are month-to-month after the first 90 days. We don't lock people in — the results do the retaining.
