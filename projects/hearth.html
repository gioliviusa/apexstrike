I

Architecture is not what we build. It's how you feel inside it.

We started Hearth with a simple observation: the rooms that people love most are rarely the most expensive ones. They are the rooms that are honest about their materials, generous with light, and unafraid of empty space.

Every project begins the same way: we spend more time listening than drawing. To how you actually live, not how you'd like to be seen living. To the way the light moves through your kitchen at 4pm. To the chairs you've had for fifteen years and can't imagine replacing.

The result is not a signature. It's a room that feels like it has always been yours — and will still feel that way in another fifteen years.

IV

How a project unfolds.

Week 01

Listen & Look

Two or three long walks through your space, at different hours. We watch how light moves before we draw a single line.

Week 03

Sketch & Discuss

Loose hand drawings and physical samples. No renders yet — models lie about light, and light is the whole point.

Week 06

Refine & Specify

Detailed drawings, joinery sections, material boards. Everything a builder will need to execute without compromise.

Ongoing

Build & Hold

We stay on site through construction. And we return — a year later, and every year after — to see what needs adjusting.
