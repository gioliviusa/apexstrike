Not interfaces. Environments. The next computing platform isn't a screen you look at — it's a space you're inside.

We design for the space between the user and the world. That means spatial interfaces that respond to gesture, gaze, and presence. Virtual environments where the laws of physics are a design choice.

Our work sits at the intersection of industrial design, computer graphics, and human-computer interaction. Every project starts with the same question: what does presence actually feel like?

01Capabilities

What we do in three dimensions.

01 — Design

Spatial Interface Design

Interfaces that live in space — voice, gaze, gesture, hand-tracking. Designed to feel inevitable, not clever.

02 — Build

XR Prototyping

Fast, functional prototypes for headsets, phones, and spatial displays. Tested with real users on real hardware.

03 — Motion

Motion Systems

Choreographed behavior across environments. Every state change is a story — with an arc, a beat, and a resolution.

04 — Audio

Spatial Audio

Sound that has position, distance, and material. We design audio environments that anchor objects to space.

05 — Visual

3D Visual Systems

Real-time rendering pipelines, custom shaders, and materials that respect the constraints of a headset.

06 — Research

Spatial Research

Original studies on how humans perceive depth, distance, and presence — published and open sourced.
